How the index stays true

The products above are one public slice of a much larger private catalog — roughly 2,250 entities across about 40 surfaces. Only the product records are shown here, and only in the public-safe form described below.

01

Authored once

Every entity in the private catalog is an authored record with a stable ID — like product.jobkit — a kind, a surface, and a status. Products are one kind among many; actions, services, capabilities, endpoints, and decisions fill out the rest of the index.

02

Status means maturity

Status is engineering maturity, not whether something is running. Shipped means built and released as a product, experimental means real but still being proven, and deprecated means kept in the index for the record, not for new work.

03

Public means projected

Nothing on this page is the private record itself. Each product is projected through a closed public shape, so fields that exist privately — paths, entrypoints, and business or ownership detail — structurally cannot appear here.

What a public record holds

Every product on this page carries the same public fields — and nothing else. Open any card above to see one in full, including its connections to other products in the set.

IDThe stable catalog identifier, e.g. product.nicos-catalog. Connections point at these IDs.
NameThe product’s public name, as authored in the catalog.
Kind“product” for everything on this page — the private index holds many other kinds.
StatusShipped, experimental, or deprecated — engineering maturity.
SummaryA short public description, drawn from the catalog record. All 85 products on this page have one.
TagsThe product’s catalog tags, with two corpus-wide markers removed in the public projection: “manual” (it was on all 85) and “product” (it just repeats the kind). The sidebar filter lists only tags used by 3 or more products; each record still shows all of its own tags.
URLA public URL, only where one is allowlisted. Just 2 of the 85 products carry one.
ConnectionsTyped links — bridges_to, affiliated_with, replaces, supports — kept only where both products are in this public set.

Design notes

This page follows the product-design philosophy behind Linear, Vercel, and Raycast: clarity over decoration, typography and spacing doing the work, a near-monochrome palette with color reserved for status. The catalog itself is modeled on Raycast’s extension store — a grid dense enough to feel like hundreds of projects, quiet enough to scan. Search and faceted filtering follow NN/g’s research on listing pages: prominent search, plainly labeled filters with counts, and applied filters you can see and remove.
